pixel-art tools · free · in your browser

SPRITE FORGE

Make sprites for your game: generate a hero or a ship and paint over it, or bring in AI renders and get back pixel art that matches your style. Nothing to install, nothing uploaded.

Hero

Roll a character, edit it pixel by pixel, then get back, side, jump and an 8-frame run cycle from it.

Ships

Generate a spaceship hull from a class and a colour set, top-down and side-on, and paint over it.

Props

Weapons, potions, chests, keys, gems, books: 16 kinds of item icon, 12 variations at a time, editable.

Tiles

Pick a material, get a seamless tileset with every edge and corner piece, ready for Godot or Tiled's terrain brush.

Snap to Style

AI renders in, game sprites out: snapped to a grid, background gone, one height and one palette. Exports a sprite sheet.
Everything runs in your browser. Made for our own games first — source on GitHub.
